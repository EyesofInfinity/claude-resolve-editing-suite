# Paid ad

An ad is judged by what people do next. It must stop the right person, make one argument, and ask for one action. Beauty helps only when it serves that.

## Starting points [default]

| Decision | Start with |
|---|---|
| Length | 15 to 30 seconds for cold traffic; up to 60 when the argument needs it |
| Shape | 9:16 and 4:5 first for phones, and 1:1 and 16:9 where placed |
| Sound | Must work with sound off. Text on screen from the first frame |
| Script | Hook, problem in the customer's words, why the usual fix fails, the answer with one proof, the offer |
| Script speed | 130 to 150 words a minute |

## Start with research

An angle is the ad's argument: the problem it starts from, the desire it speaks to, and the reason to believe. Find it in real reviews, comments and sales questions. Keep their exact words.

1. Name the awareness stage ([structure.md](../structure.md)). It decides the opening.
2. Write several angles that differ in substance. Five wordings of one idea count as one ad on many platforms. [practice]
3. Write hooks of under 8 words of different kinds: a number, a question, a callout, a quote, a before and after, a contrarian statement.

## The hook

- Stop the right person. A clickbait opening brings people who will not buy.
- Specific beats general. "$5.50. Every morning." beats "save money on coffee".
- Intrigue works and confusion does not.
- Text and motion from the first frame.

## Formats

| The viewer | Format |
|---|---|
| Sees no problem | An explainer that looks like content, with the product at the end |
| Feels the problem | Customer-style video, problem and solution |
| Knows the options | Comparison with exact numbers |
| Knows you | Offer, proof, urgency if real |

Real urgency and real proof only. Fake timers hurt. [practice]

## Picture and sound

- Native to the feed. Polish can read as an ad and get skipped, while a real person holding a real product can win.
- Big text, inside safe zones, in every placement. See [text.md](../text.md).
- Show the product working within the first third.
- Music low; voice clear; captions always.

## Test

Change one main thing at a time and log what each ad teaches:

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low hook rate (3 second views over impressions) | Work on the first frame. Around 25 percent is about average on Meta, 30 and up is strong. Varies by niche [practice] |
| Good hook, low hold | The body loses them |
| Good watch time, low click rate | Offer or call to action is weak |
| Good click rate, low conversion | The landing page |

Keep the body and swap the first scene to test hooks cheaply.

## Common failures

- Logo first.
- A list of features with no argument.
- Claims without proof, or claims the user cannot legally make.
- A desktop-sized design on a phone.
- Five variations of the same idea presented as a test.

## Ask the user

Who is buying and what stops them? What real proof exists? What is the offer? Where does the click go? Is there an ad account history to learn from?
