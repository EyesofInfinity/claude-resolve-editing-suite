# Short form: TikTok, Reels, Shorts

The feed decides in a moment. The first frame and first line are the video. People swipe with the thumb already moving.

## Starting points [default]

| Decision | Start with |
|---|---|
| Shape | 1080 by 1920, 9:16 |
| Length | The least that pays off fully, often 15 to 60 seconds. Shorts can run to 3 minutes [standard] |
| Visual change | Every 1 to 3 seconds, unless one thing happening holds by itself |
| Cut pace | `--pace tight` |
| Captions | Yes, word groups of 1 to 5, inside the safe area |
| Framing | Close. Faces and hands fill the frame |
| Rate | 30 fps is the common expected rate [practice] |

## Order

1. **First frame and first line:** the point itself. Show it. A visual hook buys permission to talk. Replacing 12 seconds of talking with one physical action is a real fix. [practice]
2. **The payoff, kept honest.** Do what the first line promised.
3. **Stop early.** Do not wind down. A loop back into the opening is a bonus when it is natural.

Voice recorded after the shoot lets you write the hook knowing what happened. [practice]

## Cutting from a long video

Choose a moment that stands alone, pays off and names who it is for. Open on the strongest sentence. Skip any wind-up. Each cut gets its own hook text. A clip must still make sense with no context.

## Picture

- Recompose for vertical. Keep the subject inside the middle. A centre crop often cuts the wrong thing.
- Keep faces clear of buttons and captions.
- Moving the crop to follow speakers or action helps. Keep moves smooth.
- Graphics and B-roll fill the frame at a large size.

## Sound

- Assume sound off for understanding, and sound on for feeling. Text must carry the meaning.
- On YouTube Shorts, music tracks reduce revenue share, so a trending track has a cost. Check current terms. [practice]
- Check levels on a phone speaker. See [sound.md](../sound.md).

## Platforms differ

Results on one platform do not predict another. YouTube Shorts leans on completion and viewed versus swiped away, and Instagram leans on shares and saves. [practice] A Short's hook is baked into the file, so it cannot be repackaged later.

## Common failures

- A slow first second.
- Wide shots that read as nothing on a phone.
- Captions under the platform's buttons.
- A widescreen film cropped in the centre.
- Asking for a follow before giving anything.

## Ask the user

What one thing happens in this video? Which platform comes first? Is it standalone, or does it lead somewhere?
