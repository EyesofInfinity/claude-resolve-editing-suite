# Sales video

The viewer is deciding whether to buy, book or apply. The video answers the questions they have and builds belief, using true claims. No fixed beat sequence is proven, so treat the structure as a list of jobs. [measured]

## Starting points [default]

| Decision | Start with |
|---|---|
| Length | 8 to 20 minutes is common for high-priced offers. Warm viewers take shorter and more direct. Cold social traffic needs a stronger hook [practice] |
| Shape | Speaker on camera with simple slides, or plain slides with voice |
| Cut pace | `--pace natural`. Polish can lower trust here |
| Captions | Yes. Many viewers start with the sound off |
| Music | Little or none |

## The jobs

1. **Hook, 5 to 30 seconds.** Name who it is for and the result. Must work with sound off.
2. **The problem and its stakes.** In the buyer's words.
3. **Why it happens: the cause they have not seen.** This is usually the centre of the video. A named myth is weaker than an explained cause. [practice]
4. **How the solution works, and why it is believable.** Show how they get there. Fear with no path forward makes people tune out. [measured]
5. **Proof.** Several kinds: results, stories, demonstrations. Credibility of the seller can be short, and proof should be large.
6. **Objections.** State one, respect it, answer it. Raising one and leaving it unanswered does harm. [measured]
7. **The offer.** What they get, and what happens next. For a call, say what happens on the call. Include any real risk reversal.
8. **The action.** One. What it costs them to wait, framed honestly, with the regret of not acting.

If time runs short, cutting filler and repetition is safe. Cutting proof is not. [practice]

Put the strongest material early. Viewers who are paying attention weigh the first arguments heavily. [measured]

## Honesty

Use only true claims, approved by the user, with disclaimers the law requires. Real urgency only. Fake timers now hurt. [practice] Never invent results or testimonials.

## Picture and cutting

- The speaker's face builds trust. Look into the lens, with clean light and a simple setting.
- Change the frame or show a slide every 30 to 90 seconds.
- Slides: one point each, big text, no bullet walls. Demonstrate on screen for software.
- Proof clips in the customers' own voices, with names, if allowed.
- Do not over-produce. A plain, honest video from the seller often fits the sale better than an agency film. This is practice and has not been shown by a controlled test.

## Sound

A close, clean voice with no music under the key claims. See [sound.md](../sound.md).

## Common failures

- A long story about the seller before any value.
- Three threats and no way forward.
- Fake scarcity.
- A price revealed with no reason to care.
- A call to action with no account of what happens next.

## Ask the user

What is the offer and price? What is the real next step? What proof can you share with permission? What do buyers doubt most? What do they say in their own words?
