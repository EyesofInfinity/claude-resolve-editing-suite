# Talking head

A person speaks to camera. The viewer stays for the person and for what they are learning. The edit protects both and stays out of the way.

## Starting points [default]

| Decision | Start with |
|---|---|
| Length | What the point needs. Teaching videos on YouTube often land at 7 to 20 minutes [practice] |
| Cut pace | `--pace natural`. `tight` for fast teaching, `relaxed` for thoughtful speakers |
| Visual change | Some change every 5 to 15 seconds in teaching: a punch-in, a graphic, B-roll. Long holds are fine when the speaker is strong |
| B-roll density | Medium, 4 to 7 clips a minute when the content is visual. Few or none on personal lines |
| Music | Quiet bed, or none. Silence under a good speaker is professional |
| Grade | Clean and natural, `Warm Interview` as a start |
| Captions | Upload a caption file for long YouTube videos. Burn in for social cuts |

## Order

1. Cold open on the promise. No greeting.
2. A few lines on why to listen and where this goes, under about 30 seconds.
3. Numbered points, each with why it matters, the build, then the payoff at its end.
4. Stop when solved. One next step.

## Cutting

- Remove false starts, retakes and long pauses. Keep the thinking pauses that give weight.
- Use a punch-in of 10 to 20 percent to hide jumps and to mark emphasis. Alternate two framings. A new crop on every sentence feels nervous.
- Cover jumps with B-roll when the line describes something visible. Keep the face on stories, opinions and emotion.
- Cut away for the length of an idea (3 to 10 seconds), then give the face back. [practice]
- Do not cut away in the first second of the hook.
- Keep visible struggle and honest moments. Being a real person often earns more trust than polish. Do not keep sloppiness. [practice]

## Picture

- Eyes in the upper third, looking into the lens. Keep the crop steady across a section.
- Fix what you can: exposure, white balance, a tilted horizon, distracting edge objects (crop past them).
- Lights behind the speaker or a lamp in frame give easy depth for a user still filming. See [picture.md](../picture.md).

## Sound

The voice is the film. Clean it first ([sound.md](../sound.md)). Music at least 10 LU under speech. Leave music out of emotional lines unless it earns its place.

## Text

Key terms and numbers only. One word on screen can do more than a sentence. Name and role once, as a lower third, for a viewer who is new.

## Suite route

`edit/README.md` to cut, `edit/to_resolve.py` for a real timeline, `animation/motion-broll/SKILL.md` for graphics timed to words, `looks/README.md` to grade.

## Common failures

- A zoom on every sentence.
- Captions over the mouth.
- Cutting every pause until the speaker sounds breathless.
- Stock B-roll of the noun just spoken.
- Music fighting the voice.

## Ask the user

What should the viewer do or know at the end? Who is watching? Is there a script, and may lines be removed?
