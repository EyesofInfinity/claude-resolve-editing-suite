# Music video and music-driven film

The song is the structure. The picture gives it a world. This is the one type where strong looks, effects and rule-breaking belong by right.

## Starting points [default]

| Decision | Start with |
|---|---|
| Length | The track, or a cut agreed with the artist |
| Clock | The song. Sections first, then phrases, then beats |
| Shot length | Varied, 1 to 4 bars in verses and much shorter in peaks |
| Grade | Whatever the song earns. Looks may change between sections |
| Sound | The track is the mix. Do not change it without permission |

## Plan from the song

1. Map the track: intro, verse, chorus, bridge, drop, ending. Write timecodes. Check the beat grid against the kick drums.
2. Give each section its own idea: a look, a place, a kind of movement or a pace. Choruses can return to a shared image.
3. Build energy across the song. Save the biggest idea for the last chorus or the drop. If the second chorus looks like the first, the film has stopped growing.
4. Decide what the video is: performance, story, concept or a mix. A concept you can say in a sentence is often the best kind.

## Cutting

- Cut on phrases more than on every beat. Cutting on every beat becomes numbing.
- Change the pattern: on the beat, then ahead of it, then long holds through several bars.
- Put hits on real musical events: a snare, a vocal entrance, a stop.
- A drop gets its biggest visual change on the first frame.
- Lip sync must be exact. Check on frames and with the user's eyes. Sync takes to the song's audio first.
- Keep the best performance takes for the emotional peaks.

## Picture and effects

- Pick one strong visual idea per section and commit.
- Effects are allowed to be the point here: `Film Stock`, `VHS`, `Glitch`, `Pixel Sort`, `Datamosh`, `Time FX`, `Beat`, `Warp`. See `EFFECTS.md`.
- Even in chaos, check what the eye follows. The artist or the main image needs to stay findable.
- Watch flash limits ([cutting.md](../cutting.md)). Strobes are common here and can harm people. Add a warning if one stays.

## Sound

Export the song unchanged, unless the user has given a new mix. Loudness matches the track. Added sound effects only in intros, outros or story moments.

## Text

Lyrics, if wanted, follow the phrasing and live in the design. Title and artist simply, early or late. Spell names the way the artist does.

## Suite route

Route D in `.claude/skills/make-video/SKILL.md` for footage. `effects/beat/README.md` for beats and beat-driven effects, `animation/animate/SKILL.md` or `builder/skills/motion-effects/SKILL.md` for animated work, `looks/README.md` for section looks, `effects/timefx/README.md` for time effects.

## Rights

Confirm the rights to the song, footage and likenesses. A cover or a commercial song can block the video on any platform.

## Common failures

- A cut on every beat, from first to last.
- The same energy throughout.
- Effects chosen without regard to the song.
- Lip sync off by a few frames.
- No idea that could be said in one sentence.

## Ask the user

What is the song about, to you? Is this performance, story or concept? Who owns the rights? What footage exists? Any videos you want this to feel like?
