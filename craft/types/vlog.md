# Vlog, travel and personal film

The viewer spends time with a person in a place. Warmth and point of view matter more than information. The trip or the day needs a shape, or it becomes a list of things that happened.

## Starting points [default]

| Decision | Start with |
|---|---|
| Length | 5 to 20 minutes for a story vlog, 1 to 3 for a travel montage |
| Cut pace | `--pace natural` for talking |
| Shot length | 1 to 4 seconds in montage, longer for a real moment |
| Music | Several tracks, changing with place or mood. Montage cut to the beat |
| Grade | One colour family that holds the feeling of the place |

## Order

1. Start with the most interesting moment or question: what is at stake today, or where this is going.
2. Give it a thread. A goal, a problem, a change. "I am trying to find..." beats "today I went to...".
3. Keep promises. If the opening sets up a question, answer it.
4. End on a real feeling or a small change, and keep the sign-off short.

Cut hard. Most of what happened does not make the film. Keep what changes things, what is funny and what is beautiful.

## Cutting

- Alternate talking with looking. Talking moves the story, and montage lets the viewer feel the place.
- Establish a new place in a few quick shots: wide, detail, a person, a sound.
- Cut montage to the music, varying length, on phrases and beats.
- Keep mistakes that are funny or honest.
- Time jumps are fine. Mark them with a hard cut, a change of light or a line.

## Picture

- Use the shot variety you have: wide, detail, hands, food, faces.
- A little handheld movement feels true, and only heavy shake needs stabilising.
- Shoot and pick for light: early and late light flatter almost everything. Choose shots at their best.
- Keep a consistent frame rate, and use slow motion from high-rate clips as a deliberate choice.

## Sound

Real sound makes a place feel present. Keep street, sea, room and laughter. Clean the talking parts. Wind noise on speech is the most common problem, so use the cleanest take and add captions if needed. See [sound.md](../sound.md).

## Text

Place names and small jokes. Consistent, simple, and few. A map or date can help a viewer follow.

## Suite route

Route D in `.claude/skills/make-video/SKILL.md`. `edit/README.md` for each talking clip, `looks/README.md` to grade. `Beat` effects add subtle moves on the beat, and `EFFECTS.md` lists what suits film looks.

## Common failures

- A list of events in order.
- A two-minute introduction to the day.
- Every shot the same size.
- One song under everything.
- Trending effects unrelated to the mood.

## Ask the user

What was the point of the day or the trip? What surprised you? Who is this for? Do you have music you are licensed to use?
