# Promo and launch film

A short film makes people want a product. It is one idea, shown fast and beautifully, with real proof. People should want to watch it twice.

## Starting points [default]

| Decision | Start with |
|---|---|
| Length | 15, 30 or 45 seconds. Over 90 needs a real reason |
| Clock | The music. Big moves on beats; 120 BPM is a common tempo |
| Shot length | 0.5 to 2 seconds, with one or two longer holds on the proof |
| Message | One sentence split across 3 to 5 beats |
| Shape | Master 16:9 and a separate 9:16 |
| Finish | 60 fps with motion blur for animated films |

## Order

1. **Hook, 0 to 3 seconds.** The strongest image first. Front-load what makes people stop. Start moving on the first frame.
2. **Problem or desire, briefly.** Show it. Do not describe it.
3. **Reveal.** The product enters through a real interaction.
4. **Proof.** One screen, number or result that shows the claim. A new business may have no numbers: one plain true fact counts, such as "roasted the day it ships". If the user has nothing true to show, shorten the film and drop the beat.
5. **Close.** A clean final state, name and one call to action, held long enough to read.

## Picture

- Use the real product. Real logo, real screens, real footage. Never invent an interface or a metric. A physical product with no footage: ask for a few daylight phone photos. Without them, draw simple shapes that clearly read as drawings, so nobody mistakes them for real packaging.
- No two beats share the same layout, and they all share one design language.
- Put the viewer's eye on one thing per beat.
- Real people, if used, for only seconds each. Treat each scene as its own designed moment.
- Frame every shot to one consistent aspect across the film. Mixed bars read as sloppy.
- Show. Avoid words naming the technique ("green screen"). Let the shot do it.
- Grade strongly for real footage, and protect brand colours. Skip the grade on a flat-colour brand film.
- Save the last frame as a poster.

## Sound

The sound is half the film. Pick the track first, then cut to it. Check the beat grid. Recorded effects as accents at the music's level. Silence before the biggest hit. See [sound.md](../sound.md).

## Variants

Make different hooks and shapes from the same data: three openings, 16:9 and 9:16, two calls to action. See [motion.md](../motion.md).

## Common failures

- Big centred words on a gradient, everything fading in.
- A slow start, with the best thing at the end.
- Five messages in 30 seconds.
- A close card gone before it can be read.
- Claims the user never approved.

## Ask the user

What is the one thing to remember? Who is it for? What real assets exist: logo, screenshots, footage, music? Where will it run?
