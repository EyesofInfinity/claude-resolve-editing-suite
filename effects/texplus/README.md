# Develop Texture Plus

The companion of Develop Texture (`effects/presets/`): eight more procedural textures, in one transform DCTL with the same sliders and blends, so the two read as one family. Built from scratch, from the things themselves. MIT License. Edit page OpenFX > ResolveFX Color > DCTL, or a DCTL plugin node; to install in `LUT\Develop\Presets` beside Develop Texture.

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| 0 Moire | two fine ring gratings laid one on the other, as two layers of sheer ribbed cloth (watered silk): centres 1.1 frame heights off the frame, the second turning slowly round the first, each layer's ribs wandering a little. The cloths' transmissions are multiplied and averaged over the pixel: the beat (the moire bands) at full strength, the ribs faint (0.15), every term kept or cut by its own frequency, all cut from 0.18 to 0.32 cycles a pixel so nothing beats against the pixel grid |
| 1 Spots | a cellular field of pigment spots: one spot a cell, each its own size and tone, rough edged, darkening gradually toward its rim as pigment gathers when a drop dries, granulated inside; spots closer than 0.08 of a cell merge (smooth minimum); the whole field warps slowly |
| 2 Tiles | glazed tiles set in grout, Shape 0 hexagon, 1 triangle, 2 diamond (60 degree rhombus), 3 eight-pointed star with crosses. The distance to the grout is exact for each tiling (the star is a square and the same square turned 45 degrees, tips meeting). Each tile its own glaze tone, a slight tilt and an orange-peel ripple; the glaze rises over each edge in a low rounded bevel and runs thin (lighter) there; the grout is matte, darker toward the tiles; the grid bends a little, as tiles set by hand do. Speed sways the light, never the tiles. Under Displace each tile is a block of pressed glass |
| 3 Loops | three sets of coiled wire seen from the side (X = A ph + B cos ph, Y = R sin ph), each set its own direction about 120 degrees from the others, its own lane spacing and loop size; the near half of each loop lit, sharp and full width, the far half thin, dim and soft, so the near half passes in front; lanes wander a little and each coil slides along its lane |
| 4 Micro | a rough specimen under a scanning electron microscope: nodules at three scales (domes joined by a smooth maximum, the finer ones growing only on the domes below them, as a cauliflower surface does), a fine roughness; shaded as secondary electrons are: yield rising with the secant of the tilt (rims and slopes bright), faces toward the detector brighter, hollows darker, a faint horizontal scan grain |
| 5 Emboss | a polished metal sheet pressed with a noise relief (beaten foil), mirroring a soft studio light from the upper left with a hot highlight and a faint sky above; the light sways with Speed |
| 6 Paint | a noise field painted in three layers of short square-ended dabs following a slow flow: each dab carries the noise's colour at its point, straight bristle streaks along it, wobbling sides, a blunt start and a tail that thins as the brush runs dry; the top two dabs over a pixel are laid in order over an underpainting of the same noise; the paint's relief is lit. Under Displace the picture bends by the dabs' colour patches (an oil-paint smear), its slope scaled to 0.4 |
| 7 Chroma Spiral | a soap film's interference colours: the film's thickness (90 to about 900 nm) a smooth noise swirled by small vortices that wind and unwind slowly; its colour the reflectance sin^2(2 pi n d / wavelength) (n 1.33) summed over 16 wavelengths against the CIE 1931 observer (the Wyman, Sloan and Shirley 2013 fit), to linear Rec.709, white-balanced; laid at 40% of its saturation |

Blend: 0 Overlay, 1 Multiply, 2 Screen, 3 Displace, as Develop Texture's: Overlay on display values centred on each texture's measured median; Multiply as a print, clear at the texture's measured 90th percentile and above; Screen exposes only the texture's lighter half, in linear, through light.h's shoulder (Chroma Spiral from 0.35, so its colours show and not only its pale higher orders); Displace sees the picture through glass pressed with the texture's relief, bilinear, up to about 40 px at 1080 and full Strength. Size, Detail, Speed, Seed, Colour, Hue as Develop Texture's. Two sliders are new:

- **Follow** (default 50): how far Overlay and Screen follow the picture's own light. Overlay's strength is multiplied by smoothstep(0, 0.5, picture luma) raised to Follow/50. Screen's added light follows the picture's light in linear terms, as a reflection does: min(1, linear luma / linear 0.6) raised to Follow/30, so at the default a night sky gets about 3% of what a sunlit one does. Follow 0 is even over the frame; above 0, black stays black. This is the family's answer to "a texture lives in the picture, never sits on top of it".
- **Shape** (Tiles only): 0 Hexagon, 1 Triangle, 2 Diamond, 3 Star and cross.

Motion: every Kind holds still in screen space or drifts deliberately by TIMELINE_FRAME_INDEX (Speed 0 holds it exactly). Moire's second cloth turns slowly (its bands sweep), Spots warp, Loops slide along their lanes, Micro's nodules shift a hair, Chroma Spiral's swirls wind and unwind on a bounded sine, Tiles, Emboss and Paint hold still while their light sways. At the default Speed the mean change from one frame to the next on grey is under 0.005 for every Kind (check.mjs).

Every cell-based Kind draws only what ends inside the 3 x 3 cells round a pixel (spots at most 0.39 of a cell from points 0.22 inside theirs; domes at most 0.75 from points 0.2 inside; dabs at most 0.97 from their point; vortices exactly zero beyond one cell, their turns summed as offsets at the pixel itself, so the bound holds), so no seam shows at a cell edge. Micro's smooth maximum is k log(1 + sum(exp(b / k) - 1)), which does not depend on which empty cells are searched.
